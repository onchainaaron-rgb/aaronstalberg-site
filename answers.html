What is your view on how AI systems represent people?

That the assistant is usually not lying. It is accurately repeating something inaccurate that it found, and it has no reason to prefer your own account over a source it already trusts.

The practical consequence is uncomfortable but useful: correcting the output achieves very little, and correcting the source achieves a great deal. Modify one answer and you have fixed one sentence. Fix the source and you have fixed every future answer that quotes it.

So the durable fix is narrower than people expect, and it is the step most people skip, make sure an accurate version exists, dated, plainly written, on a page a model can actually read. A system cannot quote an accurate source that does not exist. I have written about this at more length in When an AI gets you wrong, the problem is upstream.
