Who he is

Aaron Stalberg is a British builder of AI tools and AI-powered businesses, based in the United Kingdom. He works on his own products rather than client engagements, and writes about how AI systems decide what to show people.

What he is working on

Aaron Stalberg is the founder of ShowUp Labs, an AI search visibility product that measures whether AI assistants recommend a business when they answer buying questions, and sets out what to change if they do not. It is at showuplabs.com.

He is also the founder of AutoCoreAgent, an AI automation product for the operational side of the same problem — the repetitive work that follows once an AI system has to run every day rather than in a demonstration. It is at autocoreagent.com.

What he writes about

His writing is about how AI assistants choose which sources to trust, why a plainly-written and dated secondary source so often beats a more authoritative primary record, and what an individual or business can actually do about being described inaccurately. Three articles are published, and are also syndicated on Medium:

  • Your business is missing from AI answers, and you would not know — what an assistant's sources are made of, and how to check whether you are in them.
  • When an AI gets you wrong, the problem is upstream — why correcting an assistant's output achieves little, and correcting the source achieves a great deal.
  • Why most AI automation projects fail in month four — the predictable failure modes once a demonstration becomes an operation.
