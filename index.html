Why this matters

The shift

An assistant will recommend three or four companies for your category. If you are not one of them, nothing tells you. There is no page two.

Search gave you ten options and let you judge. Assistants give you one answer and ask you to trust it. That change is the thing most businesses have not caught up with, and it is what I spend my time measuring.
